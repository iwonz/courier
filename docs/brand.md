# Courier brand system

Courier is a dependable route for files and directories across local, SSH, browser, and webhook boundaries. The identity is energetic, technically mature, and precise without looking institutional.

## Promise

**From here to anywhere.**

Courier makes Source, Destination, policy, progress, and the verified result explicit. It does not present transfer as magic and does not claim guarantees the runtime cannot prove.

## Courier swift

Courier's swift is an aerodynamic dispatch navigator with a compact satchel, a clear forward silhouette, and controlled cobalt, coral, cream, and mint color. The tactile paper-cut rendering makes the product memorable without replacing operational clarity. It is capable and editorial: never infantile, robotic, fantasy-themed, weaponized, or derived from a third-party mascot.

Use the swift in the compact product mark, landing hero, repository banner, and optional product-surface atmosphere. Product meaning must remain complete when artwork is unavailable; the swift never replaces a label, status, or security warning.

[`courier-swift-mark-v1.webp`](../web/ui/assets/courier-swift-mark-v1.webp) is the ImageGen-authored transparent compact mark used in product chrome and favicons. It deliberately remains a raster illustration rather than an SVG approximation. Asset modules are named by surface and role rather than mascot name, so a future identity revision does not leak into application architecture.

## Visual language

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep ink | `#091A33` | Primary text and dark canvas |
| Cobalt | `#2864E8` | Product identity and active navigation |
| Warm cream | `#FFF8EC` | Illustration ground and warm contrast |
| Safety coral | `#FF704C` | Active routes, important state, and focus |
| Mint | `#7EDFC8` | Restrained orientation and verified accents |

Theme-specific coral and cobalt variants keep text and focus accessible. Success, warning, and danger use restrained semantic colors only with text or icons.

Courier uses system fonts only. Display type is tightly composed; monospace is reserved for commands, paths, identifiers, byte counters, and other operational values. Surfaces use negative space, generous rounded boundaries, clear contrast, lightweight shadows, and flowing route geometry instead of engraved texture, sharp bands, filled card stacks, telemetry decoration, fake prompts, or timestamp gutters.

## Interaction

Theme and locale are each one icon button. Theme cycles `system → light → dark`; locale cycles `English → Russian`. System and browser language remain the defaults until an explicit stored choice overrides them. Every control exposes its current and next value in a localized accessible name.

The landing uses one eager responsive inline hero picture. Installation and CLI are image-free content sections, so page flow never depends on background decoding or stitched artwork. Product surfaces may reuse the hero pair as low-opacity decoration. No artwork tracks the pointer, creates a refracted duplicate, or moves the base image. Route pulses are restrained and disappear under reduced motion.

## Voice

1. Start with the current state or required action.
2. Name the object: Source, Destination, route, archive, server, or delivery.
3. Use concrete verbs: prepare, connect, send, receive, verify, stop, retry.
4. Report only confirmed outcomes.
5. State what remains safe after failure when known.
6. Never include credentials, query secrets, or protected metadata.

Prefer “route,” “delivery,” “verifying,” “stopped,” “collision,” and “confirmed bytes.” Avoid magic-infrastructure language, fake urgency, blame, jokes in failure states, or unsupported superlatives.

## Generated asset record

The Courier swift mark and responsive hero pair were produced with the built-in image-generation workflow, then encoded as local WebP assets. Generated images contain no text, fake UI, commands, logos, credentials, watermarks, weapons, or required information.

[`provenance.json`](../web/ui/assets/provenance.json) records role, prompt, sequence metadata, dimensions, byte count, SHA-256, and generation lineage. The asset gate verifies the manifest, responsive hero pair, compact raster mark, per-file and aggregate budgets, and the absence of untracked files. Third-party package-manager and platform marks are documented in [`web/ui/NOTICE.md`](../web/ui/NOTICE.md).
