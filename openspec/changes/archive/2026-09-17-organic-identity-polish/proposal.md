# Change: Organic identity polish

## Why

The Vector release introduced correct product structure but its sharp geometric mark, independently composed section scenes, and hard-edged workbench surfaces make the landing feel tiled and reduce heading contrast. Courier needs one cohesive, readable, image-led composition rather than visibly stacked posters.

## What Changes

- Replace the deterministic SVG compact mark with an ImageGen-created transparent raster emblem that remains clear at product-chrome sizes.
- Replace three landing scene pairs with one continuous desktop/mobile panorama pair shared by the whole page.
- Remove section image masks and independent scene overlays that expose seams.
- Introduce localized contrast fields and softer rounded workbench/control geometry without changing interaction or contract behavior.
- Update provenance, budgets, tests, embedded assets, and documentation.

## Impact

This is a browser presentation and identity change only. CLI behavior, HTTP APIs, release behavior, security boundaries, routes, commands, and catalogs remain unchanged.
