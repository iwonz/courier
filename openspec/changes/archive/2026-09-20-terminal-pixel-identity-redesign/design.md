# Design

## Terminal visual system

Dark mode uses a black canvas, `#0D1015` and `#191C20` surfaces, white type, 70% white secondary type, and 55% white rules. Light mode is an accessible inversion with white canvas and 4%/9%/70%/55% black equivalents. Teal `#71FFF6` expresses action and success, yellow `#FAD14F` selection and warning, and red `#C94A55` destructive state. Light-theme teal text resolves to `#006B67`; bright teal and yellow fills always carry black text.

Controls use square geometry, one-pixel rules, two-pixel focus outlines, and instantaneous pixel-step states without chamfers, blur, soft shadows, or offsets. Pixelify Sans remains limited to the wordmark and H1/H2. Four pinned Latin/Cyrillic Overpass Mono 400/600 subsets serve every interface label, value, and command.

## Surface composition

The landing remains three naturally scrolling sections. Its hero becomes a route console with Relay, measured route path, yellow four-pixel signal, and contract-derived endpoint/route/command counts. Installation uses horizontally scrollable channel tabs and a ruled command panel. The command builder uses horizontal row rules without a table frame or inter-column divider.

Administration becomes an operations console: an admin Relay title block, real API-derived metric strip, server navigator, selected delivery facts, and policy editor separated by structural rules. Delivery becomes a transfer console with a route/status strip, explicit authorization region, and ruled manifest rows. No surface invents telemetry, monitoring data, maps, or decorative runtime facts.

## Relay v3

Five transparent v3 WebPs preserve the established silhouettes, proportions, eyes, beaks, plumage structures, earpieces, satchels, poses, and role objects. Only palette changes: cobalt becomes teal, orange/coral becomes yellow, and plumage becomes a cool neutral ramp. Neutral-v3 is the new identity authority; mark, route, delivery, and admin v3 assets reference it. A generated contact sheet verifies all five on white, black, and checkerboard backgrounds.
